How To Read The Categories

The strength of the sheet is that it separates different bet types and decision styles. Members do not need to back everything. They can stay with confidence, chase value, or mix both depending on risk tolerance.

What The Numbers Mean

The sheet becomes much easier to use once members know what each metric is trying to say. These explanations stay in plain English so the product feels approachable.

Plain-English glossary
AI Horse Confidence %

The engine's overall confidence in that runner relative to the race. Higher confidence usually means the horse fits more of the model and intelligence checks cleanly.

ML Win %

The model-estimated chance of the horse winning the race. Members use it to understand whether the win case is genuinely strong or only moderate.

Win Value %

The gap between the model's win assessment and the current market price. Positive figures suggest overlay value. Negative figures mean the market may already be too short.

ML Place %

The model-estimated chance of the horse running a place. This is especially useful for Place Bankers and for members who want steadier betting options.

Place Value %

The same overlay concept applied to the place market. It helps show when a place quote is still worth considering versus when the market has already compressed too far.

AI Recommended Units

A rough staking guide used in value-led sections. Higher units indicate a stronger edge relative to the rest of the sheet, not a guarantee of outcome.
